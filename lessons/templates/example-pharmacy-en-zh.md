# At the pharmacy | 在药店
learn: en
speak: zh
level: A1
character: Sarah | 35, pharmacist, calm and kind | female
me: a customer with a sore throat | 嗓子疼的顾客
scene: A small pharmacy near your home | 家附近的一家小药店

## Mission
- Say what the problem is | 说出你哪里不舒服
- Ask for something that helps | 请她推荐能缓解的药
- Ask how often to take it | 问一天吃几次

## Twist
She asks if you have a fever. | 她问你有没有发烧。

## Lines
opener: Hi there, how can I help? | 你好，需要什么帮助？
first: What seems to be the problem? | 哪里不舒服？
closer: Feel better soon. Bye! | 早日康复，再见！

## Phrases
- I have a sore throat. | 我嗓子疼。
- Do you have something for…? | 有治……的药吗？
- How often do I take it? | 一天吃几次？
- No, I don't have a fever. | 没有，我不发烧。
- How much is it? | 多少钱？

## Grammar
I have + a problem: I have a cold, I have a headache | 用 I have 说身体不适：I have a cold（我感冒了）
