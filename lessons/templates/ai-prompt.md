# 用 ChatGPT / Gemini 生成场景卡 · Make a scenario card with ChatGPT or Gemini

把下面整段复制到 ChatGPT 或 Gemini，只改第一行的【 】部分。把回答里的卡片复制或分享到翻译 App 的“导入场景”。
Copy everything below into ChatGPT or Gemini and change only the 【 】 line. Copy or share the card it returns into the app's "Import scenario".

---

【Scenario: going to the barber's to get a short haircut · Learn: Polish · Level: A1 · I speak: Chinese】

Write one role-play scenario card for a live voice-call language lesson. A character (played by an AI on my phone) talks with me in the language I am learning. Follow the format below exactly, with the same headings and keys, and output only the card inside one ```markdown code block.

Rules:
- learn: pl or en. speak: zh or en. level: A1 or A2.
- Every line with " | " has two sides. Left side: in Lines and Phrases, the language I am learning; everywhere else, English. Right side: my own language (Simplified Chinese if speak is zh).
- A1: present tense, very common words, sentences under 8 words. A2: past and future allowed, sentences under 12 words.
- character: name | age, job, two personality traits | male or female. Use a name that fits the country (Polish names for pl).
- Mission: exactly 3 short tasks I must do in the call. Twist: one small surprise the character brings in.
- Lines: what the character says first (opener), its first question (first), and its goodbye (closer), in the language I am learning.
- Phrases: 5 or 6 phrases I will need, natural and correct, in the language I am learning. If Polish has male/female forms, use the form for my gender and say so on the right side.
- Grammar: one grammar point the scene uses, with an example.
- Polish must be correct, natural spoken Polish with all diacritics (ą ć ę ł ń ó ś ź ż).

Format:
```markdown
# <English title> | <title in my language>
learn: pl
speak: zh
level: A1
character: <name> | <age, job, traits> | <male/female>
me: <who I am in the scene, in English> | <in my language>
scene: <where we are, one sentence in English> | <in my language>

## Mission
- <task 1 in English> | <in my language>
- <task 2> | <...>
- <task 3> | <...>

## Twist
<the surprise, in English> | <in my language>

## Lines
opener: <character's first line> | <meaning in my language>
first: <character's first question> | <meaning>
closer: <character's goodbye> | <meaning>

## Phrases
- <phrase> | <meaning and when to use it>

## Grammar
<grammar point with example, in English> | <short explanation in my language>
```
